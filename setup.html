Let’s get you started.

Have your Sparky device and invitation code ready.

  1. Connect your devicePlug Sparky in and connect it to your home network.
  2. Open local setupOn the same network, open http://sparky.local.
  3. Enter your codeUse the invitation code supplied with your device.

Having trouble? Make sure your phone or computer is on the same Wi‑Fi network as Sparky.
